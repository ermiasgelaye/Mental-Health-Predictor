What the dashboards show

  • Regional variation

    Suicide mortality differs widely between regions: the highest national rates are in Eastern Europe and the former Soviet Union (Lithuania, Russia, Belarus), Guyana and Suriname, and South Korea. See the world map.

  • Temporal trends

    This Tableau extract has suicide rates for 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015 and 2016 only. The full 2000–2021 trend is on the overview page.

  • Associations with indicators

    Social-insurance coverage (+) and a labor force with only basic education (−) show the clearest relationships; GDP and life expectancy relate weakly. No single indicator explains more than about a quarter of the variation.

  • Workplace survey

    Family history, work interference, gender and employer support relate most strongly to whether tech workers have sought treatment.

Note: these dashboards were built from the original data, before the corrections described on the overview page (e.g. company sizes shown as "05-Jan"/"25-Jun", and World Bank aggregates mixed in with countries).
