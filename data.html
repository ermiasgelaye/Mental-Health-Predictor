Data quality & limitations

Strengths

  • Standardised metrics: World Bank indicators follow international definitions.
  • Broad coverage: 217 countries, 2014–2019; suicide rates for 183.
  • Rich survey: 27 questions on treatment and workplace support.
  • Open data with transparent methods.

Limitations

  • Missing values: many indicators cover under half of countries; suicide rates only 2015–2016.
  • Reporting differences and possible under-reporting between countries.
  • Survey bias: self-selected tech workers, 60% in the US.
  • Corrected errors: Excel-mangled company sizes, impossible ages, aggregates mixed with countries.
Need the data programmatically? Both datasets are available as JSON see the API page.
